Two-label model scores

Softmax scores are not calibrated medical probabilities. N means a normal-beat annotation, not a healthy person. V means a premature ventricular contraction annotation. Every other annotation type is excluded from this experiment.

The gallery is 12 sampled N + 12 sampled V beats, plus the first eight model errors from the held-out set. It is not a prevalence sample. Full metrics below use every eligible held-out beat.

How the signal becomes a prediction

216 MLII samples at 360 Hz: 72 before the annotated beat and 144 after. Pairwise averaging reduces the window to 108 samples. Each window is centered and scaled by its own standard deviation, with a 10⁻⁶ floor. A 108 → 64 ReLU → 2 classifier learns with weighted cross-entropy and AdamW.

Limits that matter

Historical recordings, selected arrhythmias, a small subject count and only two beat labels. Bundle branch blocks, atrial premature beats, fusion and all other symbols are excluded, not identified as normal. Adjacent beat windows can overlap within a record; separation is by patient group, not random beats. Performance may differ across leads, devices and populations. No calibration, clinical validation or deployment claim is made.
